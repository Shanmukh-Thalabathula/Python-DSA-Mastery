# Practice - Conditional Statements

These exercises will help you strengthen your understanding of decision-making in Python.

---

# Easy

## 1. Positive, Negative, or Zero

Read an integer and determine whether it is:

- Positive
- Negative
- Zero

---

## 2. Even or Odd

Read an integer and determine whether it is even or odd.

---

## 3. Voting Eligibility

Read the user's age.

Display:

- Eligible to vote
- Not eligible to vote

---

## 4. Largest of Two Numbers

Read two numbers and display the larger one.

---

## 5. Divisibility Check

Determine whether a number is divisible by both 3 and 5.

---

# Medium

## 6. Largest of Three Numbers

Read three numbers and display the largest.

---

## 7. Student Grade

Assign grades using the following rules:

```text
90–100 → A
75–89  → B
60–74  → C
40–59  → D
Below 40 → F
```

---

## 8. Leap Year

Determine whether a year is a leap year.

Hint:

- Divisible by 400, or
- Divisible by 4 but not by 100.

---

## 9. BMI Classification

Read height and weight.

Calculate BMI and classify:

- Underweight
- Normal
- Overweight
- Obese

---

## 10. Movie Ticket

Determine ticket price based on age.

Example:

- Child
- Adult
- Senior Citizen

Choose appropriate age ranges and prices.

---

- Withdrawal amount

## 12. Login System

Read:

- Username
- Password

Validate the credentials.

## 13. Electricity Bill

Calculate the bill using slab-based pricing.

- Next 200 units
- Remaining units

## 14. Income Tax Calculator

Calculate tax using multiple income slabs.
