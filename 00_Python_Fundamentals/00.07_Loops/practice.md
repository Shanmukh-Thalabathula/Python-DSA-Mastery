# Practice - Loops

Practice is the key to mastering loops. Start with the easy problems and gradually work your way up.

---

# Easy

## 1. Print Numbers

Print numbers from **1 to 10** using:

- a `while` loop
- a `for` loop

---

## 2. Reverse Counting

Print numbers from **10 to 1**.

---

## 3. Even Numbers

Print all even numbers between **1 and 50**.

---

## 4. Odd Numbers

Print all odd numbers between **1 and 50**.

---

## 5. Sum of Natural Numbers

Calculate the sum of numbers from **1 to 100**.

Expected Output:

```text
5050
```

---

## 6. Multiplication Table

Read an integer and print its multiplication table up to 10.

Example:

```text
5 × 1 = 5
5 × 2 = 10
...
5 × 10 = 50
```

---

# Medium

## 7. Factorial

Read a number and calculate its factorial.

Example:

```text
5! = 120
```

---

## 8. Count Digits

Read an integer and count the number of digits.

Example:

```text
12345

Digits = 5
```

---

## 9. Reverse a Number

Read an integer and print its reverse.

Example:

```text
1234

4321
```

---

## 10. Fibonacci Series

Print the first **n** Fibonacci numbers.

---

## 11. Prime Number

Determine whether a number is prime.

---

## 12. Sum of Digits

Read an integer and calculate the sum of its digits.

Example:

```text
1234

1 + 2 + 3 + 4 = 10
```

---

# Hard

## 13. Prime Numbers in a Range

Print all prime numbers between two user-provided values.

---

## 14. Number Guessing Game

Generate a random number and repeatedly ask the user to guess it until they are correct.

---

## 15. Menu-Driven Calculator

Create a calculator using a `while` loop that repeatedly displays a menu until the user chooses **Exit**.

---

## 16. Pattern Printing

Print the following pattern:

```text
*
**
***
****
*****
```

---

## 17. Inverted Pattern

```text
*****
****
***
**
*
```

---

## 18. Floyd's Triangle

```text
1

7 8 9 10
```

---

## 19. Multiplication Matrix

Print a multiplication matrix from **1 to 10**.

---

## 20. Pascal's Triangle

Print Pascal's Triangle for a given number of rows.
